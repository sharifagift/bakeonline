Why This Matters

Most bakeries don't fail because of bad baking.
They fail because of bad business.

01
💸
You're working hard and still losing money
When you price by guesswork, your ingredients, labour, and overhead aren't fully covered. You bake more, make less. That's a system problem, not a baking problem.
02
Orders are coming in but profit isn't
Being busy is not the same as being profitable. Without understanding what each order actually costs you, growth can quietly make things worse.
03
You don't have a system — you have a WhatsApp chat
Managing orders in a notebook or messenger thread means things fall through the cracks. One clear system saves you hours every week and protects your reputation.
