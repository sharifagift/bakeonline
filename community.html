❓ Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free to join? ▼
Yes — 100% free, always. There are no hidden charges, no premium tiers, and no subscriptions. The Modern Bakers Club is our way of giving back to the baking community.
How do I get added to the WhatsApp group? ▼
After you submit the form, we'll send you a WhatsApp message with the group invite link within 24 hours. Make sure your WhatsApp number is correct!
When are the monthly training sessions? ▼
Training sessions happen once a month — dates are announced in the WhatsApp group at least a week in advance. Some are in-person in Kampala, others are online via Zoom or WhatsApp video.
Can I join if I'm outside Kampala? ▼
Absolutely! We have members from across Uganda and beyond. The WhatsApp community and online trainings are accessible from anywhere.
I'm just starting out — is the club for me? ▼
Yes! In fact, joining early is the best decision you can make. You'll learn the right way to price, manage orders, and build your business from day one — avoiding the costly mistakes most bakers make.
How do I leave the club if I want to? ▼
You can leave the WhatsApp group anytime, and you can unsubscribe from emails with one click. No hard feelings — though we'll be sad to see you go!
