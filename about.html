📖 The Problem We're Solving

Why most bakeries struggle

The bakery industry is built on skill and passion, but many bakers lack structured business systems.

Because of this, they struggle with poor pricing, low profits, burnout, and businesses that cannot grow. Most rely on guesswork instead of clear processes.

Even though demand for baked products is high, many bakeries remain financially unstable - not due to lack of customers, but due to lack of structure.

That is exactly the gap BakeOnline was built to close. Through pricing tools, business systems, practical guides, and access to affordable supplies, we give bakers the structure they need to move from guesswork to clarity, and from passion to profit.

Our mission
"We help bakers move from guesswork to clarity and from passion to profit. Because a bakery built on passion deserves to also be built on a solid business foundation."
~ Gift, Founder of BakeOnline

🎯 The problems we solve

Three real problems holding bakeries back

💰
Underpricing
Most bakers price by guesswork — with no clear picture of ingredient costs, labour, or overhead. They work hard and still lose money without knowing why.
📋
Disorganized operations
Orders tracked in notebooks, messages scattered across WhatsApp, no system for managing production or delivery. Chaos costs time, money, and customers.
📈
Inconsistent growth
Without proper business systems, growth happens by accident and not by design. BakeOnline gives bakers the structure to grow consistently and sustainably.

📅 Our Journey

From one bakery to a movement

Every milestone that brought BakeOnline to where it is today.

The Beginning
Just Cakes is born
Gift starts Just Cakes, a home bakery in Kampala built on passion, custom cake orders, and the dream of doing this full time. The business grows, but so do the operational challenges.
The Insight
The Pricing E-book that changed everything
Frustrated by lost orders and pricing confusion, Gift builds a simple step by step pricing guide, Order tracker and a pricing calculator for Just Cakes. Shares with bakers — 30 bakers request for copies within 24 hours.
The Platform
BakeOnline is launched
Recognising a gap in the market, Gift builds BakeOnline, a platform dedicated to giving bakery operators the tools, systems, and education they need to run profitable businesses.
The Community
Modern Bakers Club launches
The Modern Bakers Club WhatsApp community launches, bringing together bakers from across Uganda to share, learn, and support each other. Membership grows quickly as word spreads.
Today & Beyond
500+ bakers and growing
BakeOnline now serves hundreds of bakery operators with free tools, paid guides, live training sessions, mentorships and one-on-one consultations. The mission remains the same: make every baker a profitable baker.

💛 What We Stand For

Our values, in plain English

These aren't just words on a page. They shape every tool we build, every guide we write, and every baker we work with.

🧮
Clarity over confusion
We choose clarity over assumptions. Every decision, from pricing to production, should be guided by systems, not feelings. We believe every baker deserves to understand their numbers. No complexity - just clear, practical tools that make sense for real bakery businesses.
🤝
Community with direction.
Bakers grow faster when they grow together. We build spaces where sharing knowledge, asking questions, and supporting each other is the norm, not the exception.
🌍
Built for Uganda, by Uganda
We design tools around the real context of Ugandan bakers — local pricing, local challenges, local opportunities. Not copied from somewhere else and adapted.
📈
Profit is not optional
Passion for baking is beautiful. But Baking is a business. We believe every baker deserves to make money from their work consistently and sustainably because a bakery that doesn't make money can't survive. We help bakers understand, protect, and grow their profit consistently and unapologetically.
🎓
Education that empowers
We don’t teach theory. Built from real experience, Everything we create comes from real bakery challenges, tested solutions, and lived experience. We don't just hand over tools, we teach the thinking behind them. Bakers who understand their business make better decisions every single day.
🚀
Start small, grow bold
Every large bakery started in someone's kitchen. We meet bakers where they are. Home baker, Small shop, or Scaling fast and give them what they need for the next step. We don’t complicate what should be clear.
