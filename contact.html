❓ Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to messages? ▼
WhatsApp is our fastest channel — we usually reply within an hour during business hours. Emails are responded to within 24 hours.
Can I book a consultation through this form? ▼
Yes! Select "Consultation Booking" from the topic dropdown and tell us what you need help with. We'll follow up to confirm your session date and details.
Are the resources and templates really free? ▼
Yes — the Order Tracker, Profit Tracker, and Customer Profiling Guide are all completely free to download. No account needed.
Do you work with bakers outside Kampala? ▼
Absolutely! We work with bakers across Uganda and beyond. Consultations can be done online via Zoom or WhatsApp video call — location is no barrier.
How do I join the Modern Bakers Club? ▼
Head to the Community section on our homepage and fill in the join form. It's free to join and you'll get access to our WhatsApp group, resources, and monthly bakers meetup.
